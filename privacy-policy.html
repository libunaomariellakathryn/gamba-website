Privacy Policy

Learn how we handle your information when you use our store.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

At Gamba, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. We also want to ensure and maintain the quality of our customers' online shopping experience and the services that we are able to offer. To ensure the transparency of our data collection process, we have outlined the procedures below in our Privacy Policy.

1. Information we collect

We ask for and collect certain information from our users in order to provide our services, perform the necessary procedures to handle online transactions, and use it as a basis for communication and other relevant courses of action whenever necessary. We only receive the personal information you provide when availing certain services; this is prompted when using the Contact Form, submitting orders through our product pages, finalizing order payment and delivery, and when choosing to subscribe to our newsletter. The extent of the information we receive ends when the user stops willingly providing it through our website.

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

By using our website, you consent to the collection and use of information as outlined in this policy.

For more information on the specifics of what information we collect and how we use it, we have outlined what information is asked from the customer when availing our services.

Contact form (Contact page)

  • Personal and Contact details — In our form, we ask the user to specify their chosen topic, and to write a message based on the chosen topic.

Purpose of collection - We collect your information so that we can receive it via email. When submitting the form, the user's email software is automatically opened to fill in the recipient or "To" field based on the fixed email address, the "Subject" field based on the chosen topic from the dropdown box, and to create a draft for the body text using the message written in the message box. The user can continue to make changes to the content of the drafted email before submitting it.

How we use your Information - We will use the information we receive to respond to queries, reach out to customers, and when needed, provide any other necessary services. We will also use this to improve our products, services, and website experience.

Submitting an order (Product pages)

  • Order details — When submitting an order, we will ask the customer to specify the relevant order details by choosing from the available options. Customers can specify the item size, color, and quantity.

Purpose of collection - We collect this information so that we can receive it via email. When placing the order, the user's email software is automatically opened to fill in the recipient or "To" field based on a fixed email address, and a draft is created containing the order details for the body text based on the input received from the chosen order specifications. The user can continue to make changes and additions to the content of the drafted email before submitting it.

How we use your Information - We will use this to process your orders, communicate order updates, and provide customer support.

Additional Note: Since this is sent via email, we will also be able to keep note of the user's personal email address once we receive it in our inbox.

Transaction and Payment

  • Personal information and payment details — We will ask for information such as your full name, email address, phone number, shipping address, and payment details.

Purpose of collection - We will reach out and collect this information for a separate transaction process after receiving your order request.

How we use your Information - We will use this to finalize, process, and deliver your order/s.

Newsletter and Updates

  • Personal Information — We will ask for your full name and email address.

Purpose of collection - To send updates about our website to the user's email address

How we use your Information - We will use this to communicate changes and updates to our policy, products, services, and website.

Reminder: Please do not send us sensitive information - Our Contact form and product pages do not ask the customer to share sensitive information such as account passwords, card details, and any other personal transaction details. We will only receive the information within the drafted email when the user manually sends it; they can also choose not to send the drafted email and resume contact with us at any other time. Once your order needs to be processed and paid for, we will arrange and secure your payment through a separate method.

3. Children's Privacy Protection

This website is not intended for the use of children under the age of 18. Our policy reflects this through our data collection practices - we do not knowingly and purposefully collect personal information of someone under the age of 18. If you are under the age of 18, please avoid submitting personal or sensitive information, and only use this website under the supervision and with the assistance of a parent or guardian before availing our services.

4. User Rights

Depending on the user's place of residence (region and country), they have the right to exercise their rights when using our website. If you have any concerns regarding our policies, the way we access and handle customer information, and our practices, you may reach out to us through our email hello@examplewebsite142.com or use our Contact form. You may inquire, consult, and request to avail our services depending on these rights.

5. Information Retention

We only store the information you send through our email address for processing orders, to use it for any follow-up communication, and as a basis for handling subsequent online transaction/s. We do not intend to use it for any other purposes or activity not disclosed on our website and policies.

6. Cookies

Our website does not install cookies on your device. Cookies track your website activity and let websites remember and identify its users by saving certain information: customer data such as login details, shopping cart contents, and other personal information - it stores these as data to allow the website to improve its services by synchronizing its activity to fit repeated customer preferences and needs. Since our website does not install cookies, our website does not track and save user data unless provided through our Contact Form and when submitting an order through our product pages. Users will have to manually provide their personal information whenever prompted by our website through its services. However, this also means that our website is not able to provide a more personalized browsing experience - we won't be able to offer any recommendations regarding website information, services, and products to our users during their visit sessions.

7. Changes to our Policy

We may update our policy from time to time to better reflect our services, practices, or changes to our website. We do this in consideration of reasons related to regulatory and legal policies. The starting effect of our updated policies will be reflected by the "Last Updated" date listed at the top of the page.

8. Contact

For more information about our policy, practices, or if you simply want to reach out for any further inquiries, please contact us, your trust means everything to us.

You can contact us through our email hello@examplewebsite142.com or use our Contact form.
